# Google Ads chalay kara? Kids-fashion lead list

**Shoja uttor:** 31 October 2026 porjonto ekta Bangladeshi kids ba dress business-o **"Verified" Google advertiser hisebe confirm kora jayni**. Karon ei research session-er network policy Google Ads Transparency Center ar shob brand-er website block kore diyechhe, tai website-e `AW-` tag-o dekha jayni. Tai nicher list-er beshirbhag naam **unverified lead**: egula shotti Bangladeshi business, nijer website ache, kintu Google Ads chalay kina tar proman nai. Mattro **7 ta business-er kichu indirect proman** pawa gechhe. Egular moddhe shobcheye strong duita: **ePharma** (baby & mom section ache, ar BuiltWith-er Google Ads conversion-tag list-e or naam ache) ar **Stylebari** ("Meta Ads & Google Ads Specialist" job post). **Aarong, Bata Bangladesh, Le Reve, Apex Footwear** ar **Baby Mall BD**-er proman durbol. Kids dress-er sathe shobcheye bhalo mile nijer domain-wala kids-wear shop gula: **Clothxtra, PTex, Trends Kids, Kiddleplace, Yusha**. Kintu egular Google Ads-er kono chinho pawa jayni. Agency case study, Think with Google ba Digital Marketing Award list, kothao kono Bangladeshi kids-wear brand-er naam Google Ads-er sathe pawa jayni. Nicher 3-step self-check (Transparency Center-e domain search, View Source-e `AW-` khoja, live "Sponsored" search) diye proti domain kichu minute-ei nije confirm korte parben. Ar mone rakhben, **Bangladesh-e Google Shopping ads chalu ache kina tai niye source-gular moddhe mil nai**. Tai Shopping-e kono shop na dekhle-o se Search ba YouTube-e ad chalate pare.

## Network block-er karone shob naam ekhono "lead", proof na

Ei session theke `adstransparency.google.com` (website ar RPC endpoint duitai) ar brand homepage gula (aarong.com, lerevecraze.com, fabrilife.com, kurtiistic.com, babyshoppers.com.bd ityadi) proxy 403 / DNS error diye block hoyechhe. Transparency Center-er BigQuery copy-teo labh hoto na, karon oi dataset-e shudhu EEA ar Turkey-r data thake ([Google Cloud Community](https://www.googlecloudcommunity.com/gc/Data-Analytics/Querying-public-google-ads-transparency-center-table-doesn-t/m-p/856073)). Tai shob finding search-engine snippet theke neya, kono page puro pora jayni. Er mane **"proman nai" mane "ad chalay na" na**: check-tai kora jayni. Chaile environment-er Network access setting-e `adstransparency.google.com` ar shop-er domain gula allow kore check-ta abar chalano jay ([Claude Code docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/cloud-environments#network-access)).

| Weak | Agency-r client logo, "display ads" job, ager employee-r profile, video ad | Aarong, Bata BD, Le Reve, Apex, Baby Mall BD |

## Nije confirm korun: 3 ta check, proti domain-e kichu minute

**1. Google Ads Transparency Center (shobcheye bhalo check)**
- Chrome-e kholen `https://adstransparency.google.com/?region=BD&domain=clothxtra.com` (shesher domain-ta bodle nin). Region default-e United States thake, tai **Bangladesh** select korun ([admakeai guide](https://admakeai.com/blog/google-ads-transparency-center)).
- **Domain diye search korun, brand name diye na.** Domain search korle oi website-er shob verified account ek jaygay ashe. Company-r legal name prai brand name theke alada hoy (Aarong = BRAC, Yellow = Beximco, Sailor = Epyllion) ([Linear Design](https://lineardesign.com/blog/the-clear-path-navigating-transparency-in-google-ads/)).
- Advertiser page-e dekhun koto gula ad, **"Last shown" date**, ad-er format (text/image/video), ar platform filter (Search/YouTube/Shopping). Platform filter shudhu 4 Sep 2023-er por-er ad-e kaj kore ([Apify docs](https://apify.com/scrapesage/google-ads-transparency-scraper)).

**2. View Source-e `AW-` tag khujun**
- Shop-er website-e right-click korun, tarpor **View page source**. Ctrl+F diye `AW-` ar `googleadservices` khujun. `AW-` diye shuru ID mane website-e Google Ads tag bosano ache ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10029065)).
- Kichu na pele: Google Tag Manager (`GTM-`) tag-ke page source-e lukiye rakhte pare. Tokhon DevTools-er Network tab khule page reload korun, ar `AW-` / `googleadservices` / `conversion` diye filter korun. Conversion tag onek shomoy shudhu order-er porer "thank you" page-e thake ([BusinessTech](https://faq.businesstech.fr/faq/149)). Google-er Tag Assistant-o use korte paren ([Google](https://support.google.com/tagmanager/answer/10039345)).

**3. Live "Sponsored" spot check**
- Bangladesh theke (phone-e hole bhalo) Google-e search korun "baby dress online bd", "kids frock price in bangladesh", ba brand-er naam. Upore **"Sponsored"** label thakle mane ad ekhon live cholche ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-is-streamlining-ad-labeling-for-shopping-ads-336953)).
- Ad-er pashe ⋮ icon chaple "About this advertiser" (My Ad Center) khule, sekhane verified naam dekhay. YouTube ad-e ⓘ chapun.

Result-er mane:

| Ja dekhlen | Mane |
|---|---|
| Transparency Center-e ad ache, "Last shown" recent | **Verified**: active Google advertiser |
| Page source-e `AW-` ID ache | **Strong indirect**: tag bosano, tracking ready. Kintu taka khoroch hocche kina tar proof na |
| Search-e "Sponsored" ad dekhlen | Oi muhurte ad live. Eta shudhu ek muhurter chhobi |
| Kichu-i pelen na | Er mane ei na je ad chalay na. Advertiser verified na-o hote pare, onno legal name-e thakte pare, ba agency-r account theke ad cholte pare |

**Google Shopping note:** Google Merchant Center-er help page-e Bangladesh-er naam ache ([Google](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/16782229?hl=en)). Kintu ekta Bangla blog bole September 2026-e Google-er "Supported languages and currencies" page-e Shopping ads-er jonno Bangladesh/BDT chhilo na ([eomsbd](https://eomsbd.com/google-marketing/google-merchant-center)). Arek dike BD freelancer-ra bole fashion-er Shopping campaign theke 4x–7x ROAS ashe ([sakilmahmud.com](https://sakilmahmud.com/google-ads-for-ecommerce-bangladesh/)). Ei mil-na-thaka nije Merchant Center-e check kore nin. Shopping tab-e kono BD shop-er sponsored tile na dekhle-o shop-ta Search, Performance Max, YouTube ba Display-e ad chalate pare, tai check 1 ar 2-er upor beshi bhorsha korun.

**Paid shortcut:** BuiltWith-er puro list-e Bangladesh-er 466 ta site ache jader Google Ads conversion tag ache ([BuiltWith](https://trends.builtwith.com/analytics/Google-AdWords-Conversion/Bangladesh)). TheirStack-o prai 50 ta BD company-r list bikri kore ([TheirStack](https://theirstack.com/en/technology/google-ads/bd)). ## Conclusion

Bangladesh-er kids ar dress market-e Google Ads-er khoroch baire theke chokhe pore na. Boro brand-ra public-e Facebook campaign ar TVC dekhay, ar agency-ra fashion client-er naam gopon rakhe. Jemon Ngital ekta naam-chhara fashion e-commerce client-er golpo bole, jar Shopping ads 1.8x ROAS diye shuru hoyechhilo ([Ngital](https://ngital.com/services/google-ads)). Tai news ba award ghete Google advertiser khuje pawa jabe na. Kaj-er shongket ashe **job board ar website tag** theke. Jemon DhakaCareers-er "Google Ads" job page-e 2026-e Ruwaifi Studio, FramEmpire-er moto choto e-commerce business Google Ads-er lok khujchhe ([DhakaCareers](https://dhakacareers.com/industries/google-ads/)). Notun lead khujte oikhan theke shuru kora shobcheye druto.

Check korar order holo: age **ePharma ar Stylebari** (positive result dekhte kemon hoy, seta bujhte), tarpor **Aarong ar Le Reve**, tarpor kids specialist **Clothxtra, PTex, Trends Kids, Kiddleplace**. Kids specialist shop gulor Transparency Center khali ashle bujhben "baby dress online bd"-er moto keyword-e Google-e competition kom, mane ota ekta shujog. Tobe eta ekhon shudhu anuman, check korar porei nishchit bola jabe.
