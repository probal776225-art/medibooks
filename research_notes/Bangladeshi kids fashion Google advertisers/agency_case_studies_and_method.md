## Q4. Can the Google Ads Transparency Center be accessed via WebFetch / RPC to confirm any of these? And how can someone check any BD business's Google Ads activity themselves?

### Takeaway
**This session could not test it.** WebFetch could not reach outside hosts (4 different hosts failed with DNS/allow-list errors), so neither `https://adstransparency.google.com/?region=BD` nor the `/anji/_/rpc/SearchService/SearchSuggestions` endpoint was tried, and nothing was verified there. The BigQuery copy was also not usable (no GCP login, and community reports say it covers only the EEA and Turkey). The checks below have to be done by a person in a normal browser.

### Cited Findings
**About the Transparency Center**
- Google launched the Ads Transparency Center on 29 March 2023 as a searchable hub of ads from **verified advertisers**. You can look up an advertiser's ads, the region they were shown in, when they last ran and the format — [Google blog: launch](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ads/announcing-the-launch-of-the-new-ads-transparency-center/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/03/29/googles-new-ad-transparency-center-will-keep-track-of-a-brands-previous-ads); [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2023/03/29/google-ads-transparency-center/)
- A third-party guide says the **region control defaults to the United States** and results are only ads shown in that region, so you must switch the region to Bangladesh. It also says **searching by domain** gathers every verified account pointing to that website, while searching by name can miss sibling or reseller accounts — [admakeai guide](https://admakeai.com/blog/google-ads-transparency-center); [moda.app guide](https://moda.app/blog/google-ads-transparency-center)
- Scraper documentation (vendor claims, not verified) says ad counts shown by Google are **approximate ranges**, and **platform filters (Search/YouTube/Shopping etc.) only apply to ads shown on or after 2023-09-04** — [Apify: scrapesage actor](https://apify.com/scrapesage/google-ads-transparency-scraper)
- Another scraper developer says Google's endpoint does not support region-narrowed scraping (vendor claim, unverified) — [Apify: devilscrapes actor](https://apify.com/devilscrapes/google-ads-transparency.md)
- **BigQuery:** the table `bigquery-public-data.google_ads_transparency_center.creative_stats` exists. A Google Cloud Community reply says it holds data only for "the European Economic Area or Turkey", while the website covers all regions. One user reported "Access Denied" — [Google Cloud Community thread](https://www.googlecloudcommunity.com/gc/Data-Analytics/Querying-public-google-ads-transparency-center-table-doesn-t/m-p/856073); [ppc.land deep dive](https://ppc.land/a-deep-dive-into-the-google-ads-transparency-center/)

**About website tags**
- Google Ads tags use IDs that start with **`AW-`** (Google Analytics uses `G-`). The base tag loads from `googletagmanager.com/gtag/js?id=AW-…`, and a conversion fires `gtag('event','conversion', {'send_to': 'AW-…/…'})` — [Google Ads Help 10029065](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10029065); [Google Analytics Help 11900235](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11900235); [Troubleshoot sitewide tagging](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9148089?authuser=1)
- Tag Assistant (tagassistant.google.com) checks tags on a site. You "Add domain", and it adds a `_dbg` parameter to the URL — [Tag Assistant help](https://support.google.com/tagmanager/answer/10039345)
- A conversion tag often sits **only on the order-confirmation / thank-you page**, so you may not see it on the home page — [BusinessTech FAQ](https://faq.businesstech.fr/faq/149)

**About Shopping labels**
- Paid Shopping products carry a "Sponsored" or "Ad" label. Google has changed the wording over time (2020: moving from "Sponsored" to "Ad"), and products labelled sponsored indicate that payments may affect ranking — [Search Engine Land (2020)](https://searchengineland.com/google-is-streamlining-ad-labeling-for-shopping-ads-336953); [Consumer Watchdog](https://consumerwatchdog.org/?p=10413)
- On the Shopping tab, paid ads sit in the top row and free listings below. One April 2026 report says sponsored tiles had started appearing inside the free-listing grid (single source) — [ppc.land: free listings](https://ppc.land/free-listings/); [mulwi](https://mulwi.com/blog/google-free-listings-vs-google-shopping-ads/)

### How-to (practical steps; based on the sources above, not tested in this session)
1. **Google Ads Transparency Center (best single check)**
   - Open https://adstransparency.google.com in Chrome (desktop or phone).
   - Set the region (top right) to **Bangladesh** (or "Anywhere"). It defaults to the US.
   - Type the **website domain** (e.g. `example.com.bd`) rather than the brand name. A domain search groups all verified accounts for that site. Also try the brand name and the company's legal name (e.g. "Square Toiletries" for Supermom).
   - Open the advertiser page and check: number of ads (an approximate range), **"Last shown" date**, format (text/image/video), and the platform filter (Search, YouTube, Shopping, Display, Maps; only for ads shown since 4 Sep 2023).
   - What a result means: ads listed with a recent "Last shown" date = the business is an **active** Google advertiser. Nothing found ≠ proof of no ads: the advertiser may not be verified yet, may use a different legal name, or the ads may be run by a reseller or agency account.
2. **Website tag check (shows tracking is set up, not that money is being spent)**
   - Open the shop's website → right-click → **View page source** → Ctrl+F for `AW-` and `googletagmanager.com/gtag/js?id=AW-`. An `AW-` ID means a Google Ads tag is installed (remarketing and/or conversion tracking).
   - Or open DevTools → Network tab, reload, and filter for `AW-` / `googleadservices` / `conversion`. Add an item to the cart or open the checkout page too: conversion tags often fire only on the thank-you page.
   - Or use Google Tag Assistant (tagassistant.google.com → Add domain).
   - Note: Google Tag Manager (`GTM-`) can hide the `AW-` ID inside the container, so look in the Network tab rather than only the page source.
3. **Search / Shopping spot check (proves an ad is live right now)**
   - In Google (location Bangladesh, ideally a mobile phone in BD), search the product terms the shop sells ("baby dress online bd", "kids frock price in bangladesh", brand name).
   - Results marked **"Sponsored"** at the top (text ads) or sponsored Shopping tiles = live paid ads. Tap the ⋮ / "My Ad Center" icon on the ad to see "About this advertiser" (verified name and location).
   - Limits: this is a one-time snapshot. It depends on location, time of day, budget and device. Not seeing an ad does not prove there are none.
4. **YouTube check**: on a YouTube ad, tap ⓘ / "My Ad Center" to see the advertiser name. The Transparency Center's YouTube filter shows the history.
